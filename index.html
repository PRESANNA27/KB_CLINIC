Frequently Asked Health Questions
How often should I get a blood test if I have diabetes?
If you have diabetes, you should ideally check your HbA1c every 3 months to assess average blood glucose control. A fasting blood sugar can be done more frequently at home. Additionally, an annual kidney function test (creatinine, eGFR), lipid profile, and urine microalbumin are essential to detect diabetes-related organ damage early. Dr. Ramesh Bala at KB Clinic will guide you on the exact schedule based on your control.
What are the warning signs that I need to see an ENT specialist urgently?
You should see an ENT urgently if you experience: sudden hearing loss (this is a medical emergency and best treated within 72 hours), ear pain with discharge or bleeding, difficulty swallowing or hoarseness lasting more than 2 weeks, a neck lump or swelling, severe vertigo with vomiting, or nasal blockage with blood-stained discharge. Prof. Dr. Ganesh Bala at KB Clinic, Karaikal provides same-day endoscopic evaluation for urgent ENT concerns.
Can vertigo be cured permanently?
Yes — the most common type of vertigo, BPPV (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo), can be completely resolved in most patients with a simple, painless repositioning maneuver (Epley or Semont procedure) performed by a trained ENT specialist. Over 80% of patients at KB Clinic experience significant relief after just one session. Other causes such as Meniere's disease or vestibular neuritis require medication and vestibular rehabilitation, but respond very well to treatment when started early.
What is the difference between a General Physician and an Internal Medicine doctor?
A General Physician manages common everyday illnesses — fever, infections, hypertension, diabetes, and general health checkups. An Internal Medicine specialist (MD in General Medicine) has advanced training in managing multiple complex, chronic conditions simultaneously — such as diabetes combined with kidney disease and hypertension, thyroid disorders, metabolic syndrome, and preventive care planning. Dr. Ramesh Bala at KB Clinic holds an MD in General Medicine and a Fellowship in Diabetes (UK), giving him expertise in both roles.
Is it safe to stop my blood pressure or diabetes medicines on my own?
No — stopping blood pressure or diabetes medicines without medical supervision is dangerous. Blood pressure medicines maintain controlled readings; stopping them can cause a sudden spike leading to stroke or heart attack. Diabetes medicines maintain glucose balance; stopping them leads to hyperglycemia and long-term organ damage. If you feel well, it is because the medicines are working. Any dose changes should only be made after consulting your doctor at KB Clinic, who will base the decision on your latest lab results and clinical review.
What preventive tests should a healthy adult in Karaikal get annually?
For adults over 30 in Karaikal, the following annual panel is recommended by Dr. Ramesh Bala: Fasting blood glucose and HbA1c, complete blood count (CBC), lipid profile (cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides), thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH), kidney function tests (creatinine, eGFR, urine routine), liver function tests, blood pressure measurement, BMI and waist circumference, and an ECG for those over 40. Bala Diagnostics, co-located at KB Clinic, offers comprehensive health packages with same-day results at affordable prices.

ENT Frequently Asked Questions
What causes sudden hearing loss and can it be treated?
Sudden sensorineural hearing loss (SSNHL) is usually caused by viral infection, vascular disruption, or autoimmune events affecting the inner ear. It is a medical emergency — treatment within 72 hours with corticosteroids gives the best chance of full or partial recovery. After 2 weeks, the chances of recovery drop significantly. If you wake up with reduced hearing in one ear, visit KB Clinic immediately. Prof. Dr. Ganesh Bala provides same-day audiometric evaluation and urgent management.
Can BPPV vertigo be permanently cured?
Yes — BPPV (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo) is one of the most treatable ENT conditions. It occurs when tiny calcium crystals (otoconia) in the inner ear become displaced into the balance canals. Prof. Dr. Ganesh Bala uses the Epley or Semont repositioning maneuver to guide the crystals back into position — over 80% of patients report significant relief after just one session. No surgery, no long-term medication is needed in most cases. Recurrence can happen, but is equally treatable.
When should I see an ENT specialist rather than a general doctor?
See an ENT specialist immediately for: sudden hearing loss, ear pain with discharge, blood in saliva or nasal discharge, a neck lump that persists more than 2–3 weeks, hoarseness lasting over 2 weeks, severe or recurrent vertigo, difficulty swallowing, or chronic snoring with witnessed stopping of breathing during sleep. A general doctor can manage routine throat infections, but any of the above warrant direct ENT assessment with endoscopy — available at KB Clinic, Karaikal.
What is the connection between sinusitis and headaches?
Acute sinusitis causes genuine sinus pressure headaches — a dull, heavy pain that worsens on bending forward, located over the forehead, cheeks or behind the eyes. However, many patients diagnosed with "sinus headaches" actually have migraine — which mimics sinus pain closely. Prof. Dr. Ganesh Bala uses nasal endoscopy to determine whether sinuses are genuinely inflamed, or whether the underlying cause is vascular (migraine). Getting the correct diagnosis prevents years of ineffective sinus treatment for what is actually a neurological condition.
Does a deviated nasal septum always need surgery?
Not always. A deviated septum is extremely common and only requires surgery (septoplasty) if it causes significant breathing obstruction, recurrent sinusitis, or sleep disruption that does not respond to medical treatment. Many patients with a mild deviation can be managed with nasal saline rinses, decongestants, and anti-allergy medication. Prof. Dr. Ganesh Bala performs a thorough endoscopic evaluation to assess the degree of deviation and recommend the most appropriate — and least invasive — management plan.
When should children have a hearing test?
All newborns should have an otoacoustic emission (OAE) hearing screen ideally within 30 days. If language development seems delayed, the child does not respond to sounds, has recurrent ear infections, or breathes through the mouth and snores, an audiogram should be done promptly — regardless of age. Undetected hearing loss in early childhood significantly affects speech, language acquisition, academic performance, and social development. A.M. Audiology at KB Clinic provides paediatric audiometry for all age groups.

Internal Medicine — Your Questions Answered
How often should I check my blood sugar if I have diabetes?
HbA1c should be checked every 3 months for diabetic patients — this reflects your average glucose for the past 90 days, far more reliable than a single fasting reading. Additionally, annual kidney function (creatinine, eGFR), lipid profile, and urine microalbumin are essential to detect diabetes-related organ damage early. Dr. Ramesh Bala at KB Clinic creates a personalised monitoring schedule for every patient.
What annual tests should I do after age 30?
Dr. Ramesh Bala recommends: fasting glucose + HbA1c, complete blood count (CBC), lipid profile (LDL, HDL, triglycerides), TSH, kidney function tests (creatinine, eGFR, urine routine), liver function tests, blood pressure, BMI, and an ECG after age 40. Bala Diagnostics at KB Clinic provides all of these with same-day results at an affordable cost.
Can Type 2 diabetes be reversed or put into remission?
Yes — Type 2 diabetes can achieve full remission (normal blood sugar without medication) through 10–15% weight loss, structured low-calorie or low-carbohydrate nutrition, and regular exercise. This is most successful in patients diagnosed within the last 5 years. Dr. Ramesh Bala (F.Diab UK) specialises in metabolic reversal programs with evidence-based, personalised plans tailored to each patient's profile.
What are the earliest warning signs of kidney disease?
CKD is called a "silent disease" because 70% of kidney function can be lost before symptoms appear. Early warning signs include foamy or bubbly urine (protein leakage), swelling of feet or around eyes, persistent fatigue, and reduced urine output. Anyone with diabetes or hypertension for over 5 years should have an annual eGFR and urine microalbumin test. KB Clinic's structured CKD monitoring can detect kidney damage years before it becomes symptomatic.
Is thyroid disease lifelong? Can it be cured?
Hypothyroidism requires lifelong thyroxine replacement, but patients live completely normal symptom-free lives with the correct dose. Hyperthyroidism (Graves' disease) can sometimes achieve remission after 12–18 months of anti-thyroid medication. With proper management, thyroid disease does not limit quality of life. Dr. Ramesh Bala at KB Clinic monitors thyroid function with on-site TSH testing at Bala Diagnostics and adjusts doses proactively.

Bala Diagnostics
Clinical Laboratory
Bala Diagnostics is KB Clinic's dedicated in-house laboratory offering a comprehensive suite of pathology, biochemistry, haematology, and microbiology tests — with same-day results for most investigations. Our lab meets the highest quality standards to support accurate diagnosis.
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC), ESR, and haematology panel
  • Diabetes: HbA1c, fasting & post-prandial glucose, insulin levels
  • Renal & liver function tests — same-day results
  • Thyroid profile: TSH, T3, T4, anti-TPO
  • Lipid profile, cardiac markers, uric acid
  • Home sample collection available — call 89259 29922
A.M. Audiology & Hearing Aid Centre
Hearing Rehabilitation
A.M. Audiology & Hearing Aid Centre provides expert audiological evaluation, hearing aid fitting, and complete after-sales service. Working closely with Dr. Ganesh Bala, our audiologist delivers precise hearing rehabilitation for patients of all ages — from children to the elderly.
  • Pure-tone audiometry & speech audiometry testing
  • Digital & rechargeable hearing aid fitting from leading brands
  • Custom ear mould fabrication for optimal fit and comfort
  • Hearing aid programming, fine-tuning, and regular servicing
  • Paediatric hearing assessment and rehabilitation
  • Tinnitus management counselling and devices
